# Fonts

All fonts here are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org).

- `concrete*.woff2`, `typewriter.woff2`: Computer Modern Unicode (CM Concrete, CM Typewriter), after the
  Computer Modern and Concrete Roman typefaces of Donald E. Knuth; Unicode version by Andrey V. Panov. Subset.
- `euler.woff2`: Euler Math, after the AMS Euler typeface of Hermann Zapf; OpenType version by Khaled Hosny. Subset.
- `../../src/hand.json`: strokes of EMS Readability by Sheldon B. Michaels (SVG conversion by Windell H. Oskay),
  derived from Source Sans Pro by Paul D. Hunt, Adobe. Converted to stroke lists; a few math signs added in the
  same proportions.
